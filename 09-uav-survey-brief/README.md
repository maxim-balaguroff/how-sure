# Geological brief for a UAV magnetic survey

English · [Русский](README.ru.md)

A template the geologist fills before any survey design and gives to a licensed survey operator: the geological question and the decision, the target and its expected response, the survey requirements, the accepted data, the stop points, and who is responsible for what. Each field answers one question the operator would otherwise have to ask.

The filled example uses the made-up dyke of [tool 02](../02-magnetic-survey-check/). Every computed number in it is recomputed by `check_numbers.py` (ALL 44 PASS).

Files: PDF [EN](uav-survey-brief.pdf) [RU](uav-survey-brief.ru.pdf) · Word template [EN](uav-survey-brief.docx) [RU](uav-survey-brief.ru.docx)

This brief belongs to a proposed programme: no flights and no surveys have been made. A licensed operator carries out the survey. My role is the geological problem statement and the interpretation.

Built with AI assistance; the geology and the checks are mine.
